Most charts look chaotic until you know what to wait for.

This is the one setup we trade, in 5 steps:

1. The range: price goes sideways in a tight box
2. The break: a candle body closes outside it
3. The zone: from that candle's close to its wick
4. The retest: price comes back into the zone, that is the entry
5. The risk: stop beyond the range, size from your risk %

No indicators stacked on top of each other. No guessing. Just the same five questions on every chart.
