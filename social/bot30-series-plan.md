# 30 days with the bot · series plan

**The story:** "I get 20 minutes a day to trade. So I wrote my rules into a bot." 30 days on a **paper account**, every trade shown, wins and losses. Each day is a short video (15–25 s). Faceless: screen recordings + your voice or text.

## Before day 1
- Pick ONE paper account and note the start balance (screenshot it: that is your proof on day 30).
- Pick the system and timeframes, and keep them the same for 30 days (changing rules mid-way kills the story).
- Turn the bot on: terminal → 🤖 → timeframes → risk 0.5 % → Turn auto ON. Keep the terminal open on a PC or VPS.
- Post video 0 (the teaser): `social/bot30-day01/`.

## Every day (10–15 minutes)
1. Open the terminal → 🤖 → *Settings / log*: what did the bot do today?
2. Screen-record (phone, vertical) the chart of today's trade, or the setup it was waiting for.
3. Make the video in CapCut with this template:

| Part | Seconds | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| Day tag | 0–2 | **"Day 7 / 30"** + one-line hook ("It skipped 3 setups today. Here's why.") |
| What happened | 2–15 | the chart: range → zone → limit → result |
| The number | 15–20 | **"Paper account: +1.2 % / −0.5 %"** (running total) |
| Next | 20–22 | "Day 8 tomorrow." |

4. Post with the same 3–5 hashtags + "paper account · not financial advice".

## Hooks that keep people watching
- No trade today: "Day 4: the bot did nothing. That's the point."
- A loss: "Day 9: first loss. Here's exactly where it went wrong."
- A win: "Day 12: it took a trade while I was at the playground."
- Milestones: day 7, 14, 21: "One week in: the full numbers."
- Day 30: the result, the equity curve, what you learned. Then: "Want to see how it works? Link in bio."

## Rules for yourself
- Post every day, even boring days. Missing days breaks the series.
- Always show the real number, also when it is down.
- Never say "signals", never promise results, always "paper account · not financial advice".
- Answer every comment in the first hour.
