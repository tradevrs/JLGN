# Growth plan · getting the first VIP members

## 1. Fill something free first, not VIP
Nobody joins an empty paid room, but people do join free things. You already have them:
- **Free Playbook**, **VRS Indicator Lite**, the **free Discord**, the **monthly paper challenge** (leaderboard, top 3 win Pro).
- Order: **content → free thing → trial → VIP**.
- The challenge leaderboard keeps the community looking active even with few people, and winners end up in VIP.

## 2. Make "small" a selling point
- **Founding members**: first 50 seats, price locked for life, direct access to you. Small = exclusive, if you say it honestly.
- **Hand-pick the first 10–20**: traders you know, people you helped first in trading Discords / Reddit (help, don't post links).
- Give them **a free VIP month** for honest feedback and, if they like it, a review. They become your first proof.

## 3. Content without going live (≈ 3 hours a week)
- Faceless works for trading: screen recordings + your voice or on-screen text.
- **Batch**: one evening a week, record 5–7 short videos, schedule them.
- Instead of lives:
  - a weekly recorded **chart of the week** (5 min)
  - during London: 2–3 chart screenshots in the Discord from your phone ("VA2 forming on gold, waiting for the return")
  - later: a fixed 15-minute live, same time every week (e.g. after bedtime)

## 4. One video, three platforms
TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts get the same file.

## 5. A simple week
| Day | What | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | record 5–7 short videos | 1.5 h (evening) |
| Every day | 1 short video goes out (scheduled) + answer comments/DMs | 10 min |
| 2× a week | 1 Instagram carousel | 30 min |
| During London | 2–3 chart screenshots in the Discord | 5 min |
| Friday | chart of the week | 20 min |

## Rules
- Never "signals", never promise profit, always "educational · not financial advice".
- Show losses too: honesty is what makes people trust you enough to pay.
