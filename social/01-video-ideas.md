# Video ideas

## ⭐ 1. "I only get 20 minutes a day to trade" · 30-day bot series (CHOSEN)
You're a parent with little time, so you wrote your rules into a bot and let it trade a paper account for 30 days. Every video is "Day X".
- Honest and relatable, gives people a reason to follow (what happens on day 30?), shows the bot without selling, faceless.
- Video 0 (teaser) is made: `bot30-day01/`. Plan: `bot30-series-plan.md`.

## 2. "Wait. Wait. Wait. Now." · silent satisfying loop
~12 seconds, no voice, made to loop. The chart plays, soft text appears: wait… wait… wait… while the range forms and price runs away. Then the zone lights up, price returns, a *click*: **Now.**
- Calm, different from loud trading content; loops get rewatched (more reach). Comments ask "what's the strategy?" → next video.
- Effort: lowest. One screen recording + a few text pop-ups and sounds.

## 3. "Two traders. Same chart. One rule."
Split screen, same gold chart. Left "Impatient trader" buys the breakout, gets stopped ❌. Right "VRS trader" waits for the return, enters ✅. End: **"The only difference: one waited."**
- Instant visual story, very shareable. Use a real example, and say in the caption it doesn't always go like this.

## 4. Method explainer (35 s)
"We don't trade breakouts. We trade where value moved." Value Area → shift → origin zone → return → stop beyond value. Same story as the first carousel.

## Recurring formats for later
- **VA1 or VA2?** show a chart, pause, reveal (comments = reach)
- **Mistake of the week** (honesty builds trust)
- **We tested it** (real Backtest Lab results)
- **This setup in 30 seconds** (the zone appearing on a real chart)
