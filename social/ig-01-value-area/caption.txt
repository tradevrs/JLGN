We don't trade breakouts. We trade where value moved.

Every move starts in a Value Area: the place where buyers and sellers agreed on price. Our whole method is about what happens when that agreement breaks.

VA1 · the first shift against the trend. The market changes its mind.
VA2 · the next shift in the same direction. The market confirms it.

Neither is the entry. The entry is the Origin zone: the candle that left value. We wait for price to leave it, then come back. Most traders are gone by then.

And if price gets back inside value, the idea is dead. That's where the stop goes. No hoping.

Every rule in this method was tested before we trade it, and only what held up on data it was never tuned on made the cut.
