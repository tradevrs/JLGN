Gold M15 TR1 Playbook

One signal, one filter, one exit. H1 tells you which way to trade and when to get out. M15 gives you the entry and moves your stop.

Side and exit

M15
Entry and stop

Enter on a TR1 retest. Move the stop up after each new BOS.

The rules

Five rules, in the order you use them. Nothing else is part of the system.

  1. 1
    SideH1

    Trade only in the direction of the last H1 break.

    Last H1 body close through a range was up: buys only. Down: sells only.

  2. 2
    SignalM15

    Take TR1 only: the first M15 break in a new direction, pointing the H1 way.

    This is the moment an M15 pullback ends and M15 turns back with H1. The range needs at least 12 candles and can be no wider than 2.5× ATR(14). A break is a body close outside it.

  3. 3
    EntryM15

    Limit order at the breaking candle's body close. Stop beyond the range plus 0.1× ATR.

    Price must first leave the zone, then come back within 50 candles (12.5 hours). No fill by then: cancel. No take profit.

  4. 4
    ManageM15

    After each new M15 BOS in your direction, move the stop to that range's stop.

    Only ever tighten it. No breakeven, no partial closes.

  5. 5
    ExitH1

    Close when H1 breaks the other way.

    An H1 body close through a range against you. That is the only target; the trailed stop is the only other way out.

When to stop

Rules for you, not for the market.

  • −20R from your equity peakNo new trades. Re-run the Lab on fresh data before restarting.
  • Every 25 tradesCompare your live R per trade with the Lab's.
  • 40 live trades below 0R per tradeStop and re-test. The edge may be gone.
  • Changing a ruleOnly after a Lab test, never in the middle of a drawdown.

Going live

    The Lab gave +0.64R over 80 trades for this exact system, but no drawdown, unseen or quarter results. Run it once with the settings below. Keep it if the unseen 30% is above 0R and at least 3 of 4 quarters are positive. If not, use the Lab pick: the same system with the H1 filter off.

  2. Demo for 30 trades

    About four months. Let auto-trade (🤖) run it so fills and stop moves match the Lab exactly. Log each trade: R, session, H1 direction.

  3. Go live small

    Only if the demo shows more than 0R per trade and the bot did what the Lab would have done. Start on the lowest risk row you are comfortable with.
